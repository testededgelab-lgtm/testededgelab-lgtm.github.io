How I test

Four checks before any result counts

Most backtests you see online skip these. That is why so many "90% win rate" systems lose money live.

Check 1

Real costs

Brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, stamp duty and slippage. On short timeframes, costs decide the result.

Check 2

Two halves

The edge must hold in both the first and second half of the data, or it was luck.

Check 3

Random entries

Same number of trades, same holding time, random days. The strategy must beat most of those runs.

Check 4

Paper before live

Every bot runs on paper first. Live trading starts only when you switch it on.
